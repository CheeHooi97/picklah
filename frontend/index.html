Use food, movie, and game templates or add your own choices. Customize your wheel with colors, emojis, and images. Your drafts stay on your device.

A wheel picker for everyday decisions

PickLah is a random picker wheel: add the choices you are considering, then spin to select one. Use it as a random name picker for a group, a food wheel when you cannot decide what to eat, or a decision wheel for movies, games and tasks.

How to use the picker wheel

  1. Open Choices and add one option per entry. You can type names, paste a list, or import the first column of a CSV or Excel file.
  2. Give your wheel a title and adjust its colours, emojis or images.
  3. Return to Wheel and tap Spin to pick a result.
  4. To pick a sequence without repeats, enable Remove winners from the next spin. Restart the round to include everyone again.

Read the wheel picker guide for name picking, food decisions and choosing without repeats.

Do I need an account?

No. You can edit and spin without signing in. Your draft is saved on this device; signing in does not upload it.

Can the same choice win twice?

Yes, by default every choice stays in the wheel. Turn on the remove-winners option to pick without replacement. Two entries with the same name are still two separate choices.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The website works on phones, tablets and computers. On a phone, use the Wheel and Choices tabs to switch between spinning and editing.
