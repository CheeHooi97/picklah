A Malaysian food wheel for your next meal

PickLah helps you choose from food you already want to eat. Start with pan mee, nasi lemak, roti canai, chicken rice, bak kut teh, satay, sushi and burger, or replace them with your own shortlist.

How to choose lunch with a wheel

  1. Select Use Malaysian food choices to apply the food template. If you have a saved wheel, it stays until you choose to replace it; Undo restores the previous wheel.
  2. Open Choices and remove anything outside your budget or dietary needs. Add food or places you actually want and can get nearby.
  3. Open Wheel and tap Spin. PickLah chooses one entry from your list.

For example, shortlist nasi lemak, pan mee, chicken rice and roti canai for lunch. For a group, agree on suitable options before spinning. PickLah does not find nearby restaurants, check opening hours or verify dietary or halal suitability.

Can I change the food choices?

Yes. Type your own options, paste a list or import a TXT, CSV or Excel file. For spreadsheets, the first column of the first worksheet is used. Review the preview and remove headers before adding entries.

Can the same meal win again?

Yes by default. Enable Remove winners from the next spin to work through the list without repeats. Restart round restores all entries.

How does the food wheel choose?

The browser's cryptographic random generator chooses one eligible entry, then the animation lands on it. Each eligible entry has the same chance. Duplicate labels are separate entries.

Do I need an account?

No. Editing and spinning work without signing in. Your draft stays on this device; signing in does not upload it.

Can I share my food wheel?

Publishing new shared wheels is currently marked coming soon in the website. Existing shared links can still open. Content in a published wheel is stored on the server and visible to anyone with its link.
